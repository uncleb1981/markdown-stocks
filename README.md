# The Reset

The price broke. The fundamentals didn't. The Reset shows a small daily list of stocks that dropped 10-30% in the past month but still pass the board's checklist: P/E and forward P/E under 22, PEG under 1, net profit margin over 5%, return on equity over 10%, RSI under 45, 5-day average volume under 80% of its 30-day average, market cap over $1 billion, positive EPS growth (and revenue growth when reported), and no banks or pharmaceutical companies. Whatever clears the checklist gets ranked by a 0-100 Reset Score — not market cap — rewarding stronger fundamentals, reasonable valuation, a decline that's meaningful but not catastrophic, deeper oversold RSI, and volume that's cooled off more sharply. Some days nothing clears every check; that's the bar working as intended.

Swipe (or tap Pass/Back) through each day's picks. Backed names go to a local Boardroom list. Each card also shows its next earnings date and the RSI level that would need to clear before the pick stops counting as oversold — objective checkpoints instead of a suggested holding period.

**Informational only — not investment advice.** See the in-app FAQ for the full methodology.
