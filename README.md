# Haircut

A small daily list of stocks that dropped 10%+ in the past month but still pass a value-and-quality screen: P/E and forward P/E under 22, PEG under 2, net profit margin over 3%, return on equity over 5%, RSI under 45, relative volume under 1x its 30-day average, and market cap over $300M. The top 7 by market cap make the daily cut.

Swipe (or tap Pass/Watch) through each day's picks. Saved names go to a local watchlist.

**Informational only — not investment advice.** See the in-app FAQ for the full methodology.

Live site: https://uncleb1981.github.io/markdown-stocks/

`cards.json` refreshes automatically on weekday mornings from live market data.
