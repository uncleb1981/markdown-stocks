FAQ

What is this?
A small daily list of stocks that dropped 10%+ in the past month but still pass a basic value-and-quality screen. It's a personal research tool, not a stock-picking service.
How are the 7 picks chosen?
Every name has to pass all of these: down 10%+ in the last month · P/E and forward P/E under 22 · PEG under 2 (cheap relative to its growth rate, not just cheap) · net profit margin over 3% and return on equity over 5% (rules out financially fragile companies) · RSI under 45 (actually oversold, not just recently down) · trading volume under its own 30-day average (hasn't been widely noticed yet) · market cap over $300M. Whatever clears all seven filters gets ranked by market cap, and the top 7 make the cut.
Where does the explanation under each stock come from?
It's built from real reported earnings — beat or miss vs. estimates, and the report date — not a news search. A beat that still sold off is flagged as likely guidance-driven; a miss is called out directly. It won't catch a specific event (a downgrade, a lawsuit, a recall) unless that shows up in the earnings numbers themselves.
Is this investment advice?
No. It's informational only, not a licensed recommendation, and it never places trades on its own.
How often does it update?
Weekdays at 8:10am, automatically, using live market data.
